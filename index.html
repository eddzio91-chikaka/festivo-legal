Festivo Privacy Policy

Privacy-First & No Tracking

Effective Date: November 4, 2026

Festivo ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed with privacy as a foundational principle. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when using our mobile application.

1. Information Collection & Use

Festivo does not collect, transmit, sell, or monetize any personally identifiable information (PII). The App functions as a client-side utility.

  • Location Data: If you explicitly grant permission using the "Use my location" feature, your device's approximate or precise location coordinates are accessed temporarily solely to detect your current country and retrieve relevant public holidays. This location data is processed locally on your device and is discarded immediately after country detection. It is never transmitted to our servers or stored remotely.
  • Saved Trips & Preferences: Any holidays or trips saved via the heart icon remain stored strictly inside your device's local Room database and never leave your hardware.

2. Third-Party Services & APIs

To provide holiday data and mapping capabilities, the App communicates with the following external endpoints:

  • Nager.Date API: Used to fetch public holiday calendars by country code. No user identity or tracking parameters are sent with these requests.
  • OpenStreetMap (osmdroid): Used to render map tiles when reviewing locations. Map data is © OpenStreetMap contributors under the Open Database License (ODbL).
  • FlagsAPI: Used to display standard country flag graphics.

3. Analytics and Advertising

Festivo contains no third-party analytics SDKs, no behavioral trackers, and no advertising networks. We do not track your searches, session times, or user behavior.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all trip data is stored in your device's internal SQLite database, you retain complete control over your data at all times. You can remove individual saved entries using the in-app delete button or wipe all data instantly by clearing the app storage or uninstalling the application.

5. Children's Privacy

Festivo does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. The application does not collect personal data from any user.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via GitHub: github.com/eddzio91-chikaka.
